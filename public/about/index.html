About Certonymity

We are building what the internet should have been: privacy-first, decentralised, and accountable.

Our first product, c.Email, allows users to send and receive end-to-end encrypted emails without relying on third parties or complex setups. Combining advanced cryptography with real-world identity verification, c.Email lets people send private and safe messages from their existing email address – providing both freedom from surveillance and protection from fraud.

Beyond email, Certonymity was founded to solve the fundamental problem of digital identity: the tension between the individual right to privacy and freedom, and society's need for accountability and regulation. We are creating the technology to solve this: a system for digital identity that provides anonymity by default and accountability when necessary.

Collectively, our team has spent decades building successful businesses and software focused on technology and financial services. We are actively developing a full range of privacy-focused products to make the internet better for everyone who uses it — everywhere from communications to payments to proving your identity.
